Privacy Policy - Merge Monsters: Last Stand

Last updated: 28 October 2026 simplifyBytes ("we") publishes the mobile game Merge Monsters: Last Stand (the "Game") on Google Play.

Data the Game itself handles

The Game does not require an account. Game progress (levels, stars, Essence, upgrades, scores, unlocked monsters, daily-reward streak and settings) is stored only on your device and can be erased from the Game's Settings ("Reset progress") or by uninstalling the Game. We do not operate our own servers.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows advertisements (a small banner, interstitials between rounds and an optional rewarded video) through Google AdMob (Google LLC). To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google: your device's advertising identifier, coarse device and app information, ad interaction, crash and diagnostic data, and approximate location derived from your IP address. Google's use of this data is described at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

Leaderboards (Google Play Games Services)

Leaderboards are optional. If you choose to sign in with your Google account through Google Play Games Services, Google processes your Play Games player name, avatar and player ID, and the scores you submit are stored by Google and shown to other players on the leaderboards. You can play without signing in; local records are then kept only on your device. Google's handling of this data is described at https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can remove Play Games data for this Game from your Google account settings (Play Games > Settings > Delete Play Games account & data).

Consent and your choices

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the Game shows a consent form through Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested. You can change your choices at any time from the Game's Settings ("Ad privacy choices"). You can also limit ad personalisation or reset your advertising ID in your device's Google settings. Declining consent does not limit the Game.

Reminders (notifications)

The Game can show one local reminder a day (for example when your daily reward streak is about to end). Reminders are scheduled on your device only; no data is sent anywhere for them, and you can turn them off at any time in the Game's Settings ("Daily reminder") or in your device's notification settings.

In-app purchase (Remove Ads)

The Game offers one optional purchase that removes banner and pop-up ads. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy; we never receive your card or payment details. The Game only stores on your device that ads were removed, and asks Google Play on start-up whether you own the purchase so it is restored after a reinstall. Optional reward videos remain available.

Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security and retention

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections. We retain no personal data ourselves. Data processed by Google is retained according to Google's policies.

Changes

We may update this policy; the latest version is always available at this address.
